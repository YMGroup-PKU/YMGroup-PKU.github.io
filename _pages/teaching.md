## Graduate Course

**计算流体力学基础 / Foundation of Computational Fluid Dynamics**

- **Offered:** Fall 2021
- **Level:** Graduate (Fluid Mechanics, Aerospace, Energy & Environment)
- **Category:** Elective
- **Credits:** 3

This course is designed for graduate students in fluid mechanics who did not study computational fluid dynamics (or even fluid mechanics) at the undergraduate level. It serves as a preparatory course for the advanced computational fluid dynamics course in the following semester, focusing on fundamental concepts that will be used in the advanced course but cannot be covered in detail there. Topics include finite difference and finite volume methods, numerical schemes for hyperbolic conservation laws, and an introduction to Riemann solvers.

## Undergraduate Course

**工程流体力学 / Engineering Fluid Mechanics**

- **Offered:** Spring 2022, Fall 2022
- **Level:** Undergraduate (Aerospace Class of 2019; Mechanics Class of 2020)
- **Category:** Required
- **Credits:** 3

This course introduces the fundamental principles and analytical methods of fluid mechanics, with an emphasis on engineering applications. It focuses on developing a solid grasp of basic concepts and methods, qualitative understanding of fluid flows, and fluid-mechanics thinking. The course covers fluid statics, kinematics, integral and differential forms of conservation laws, dimensional analysis, pipe flows, and boundary layers. Instruction is primarily lecture-based with supplementary multimedia materials.
